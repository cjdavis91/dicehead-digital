09

Quick answers

How long does a build take?

48 to 72 hours once payment clears.

Do I pick my exact laptop?

You pick the tier and specs. We source a machine that matches, build it, and test it.

What if my computer does not qualify for Diced Out?

Then there is no Diced Out, and your $75 booking fee rolls into a built machine instead. You never lose it.

What does the $75 booking fee cover?

It covers the work of finding and checking your machine. It is included in every machine and Diced Out total. It is non-refundable. Single plugin installs have no booking fee.

What is a single plugin install?

$25 flat, remote, no booking fee. CJ installs one plugin on your computer, authorizes it, and checks it loads in your DAW. Tell him which plugin when you order. He confirms it is available before you pay anything.

Do you build Macs?

Yes. MacBook Pro builds.

What does the warranty cover?

90 days on the machine and the build, from pickup. It covers parts CJ sold and work CJ installed. It does not cover drops, spills, or changes you make after pickup.
